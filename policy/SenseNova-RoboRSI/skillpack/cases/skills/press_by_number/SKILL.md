# Press by number

Read both current cards and identify the left red, right red and blue buttons. Record the two target counts before touching a button. Public demonstrations follow left red count, blue once, right red count, blue once; this is a demonstrated sequence, so check the current instruction and observations rather than assuming a hidden rule.

For every button press, press vertically until the cap is visibly fully depressed, hold briefly, then lift clear and let the cap fully rebound before the next press. Avoid shallow taps or consecutive presses without full release. Centre each press on the observed button. Count a press only after a visible downstroke **and** rebound following retreat. Keep a ledger of confirmed cycles for each button; a command or brief contact alone is not a count. If a press misses or rebound is unclear, inspect the head and wrist views before deciding whether to retry. Extra presses may be irreversible; do not add one from a guessed count.

After the last confirmation, verify both cards are unchanged, all buttons have rebounded and no hand still touches them. Open and withdraw both arms as the demonstrated endings show, then check native `done`.
